Apple Inc. Strategy Capstone

Business Policy & Strategy, Rutgers University · Summer 2026 · Group project

Overview

A multi-part capstone where our team analyzed Apple's industry, competitive position, and biggest strategic challenges, then made recommendations for where the company should go next.

My role

I owned Part A, which set the foundation for the rest of the project:

  • Industry overview of the consumer tech and smartphone market
  • Porter's Five Forces analysis of competitive pressure on Apple
  • SWOT analysis of Apple's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats

Key findings

  • Apple's biggest risks are gaps in AI capability compared to competitors and heavy reliance on iPhone revenue
  • Recommendations were built around a VRIO analysis of which resources give Apple a lasting advantage
