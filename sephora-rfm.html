Sephora Customer RFM Analysis

Data analytics project · Python and Excel

Overview

Used a Sephora customer dataset to figure out which customers are the most valuable and which ones are slipping away, so marketing can target each group differently.

What I did

  • Scored every customer on Recency (how recently they bought), Frequency (how often), and Monetary value (how much they spend)
  • Built the scoring logic in Python and Excel to group customers into segments
  • Wrote a short profile for each segment describing who they are and how to market to them

Why it matters

RFM turns raw transaction data into groups a marketing team can actually act on, like rewarding loyal big spenders or winning back customers who haven't bought in a while.
