Arsenal FC AI Marketing Agent

AI in Marketing · Build a Marketing Agent assignment

Overview

Built an AI agent that writes marketing content as Arsenal FC, including social captions, emails, ads, blog posts, and matchday scripts, all in the club's brand voice.

How the voice works

  • Confident without bragging: state the facts and let results speak
  • Use specific details like scorelines, stats, and player names instead of vague hype
  • Save the big emotion for moments that earn it, like trophies and historic wins
  • Respect the audience and never explain why something is exciting

Testing

Refined the agent by reviewing its output across five scenarios: a routine win, a big win over a rival, a milestone goal, a tough away loss, and a historic title win. Adjusted the voice so captions had more feeling while staying on brand.
